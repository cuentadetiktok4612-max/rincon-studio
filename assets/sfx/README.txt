SFX — El Rincón de lo Desconocido

The app keeps a small fallback bank of locally synthesized WAV sounds and now also includes six trimmed sound effects supplied by the user. The manifest is assets/sfx/sfx.json.

The real recordings are used selectively:
- Real whoosh: opening and selected wipe transitions.
- Grainy swish / zoom transition: only a few selected visual transitions.
- Funny slide: only for light/comedic narration that warrants it.
- Mouse click: the two end-card CTA actions (Like and bell).
- Sword swish: only when narration mentions sword/combat imagery.

The mix avoids stacking multiple effects at every cut. Voice remains in front, the background tonal bed is very quiet, and repetitive synthetic melody notes were removed. If an external file is missing or cannot be decoded, the app uses a quiet procedural fallback where appropriate.
